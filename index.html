Reference

How to fold the paper

Six steps from a square sheet to a 12-layer wedge. Every line you draw above is a cut through all twelve layers.

  1. Fold corner to corner

    Start with a square. Fold it in half along the diagonal to make a triangle.

  2. Fold it in half

    Bring the two pointy ends together. The right-angle corner is now the centre of your snowflake.

  3. Mark thirds

    Point the centre corner down. Imagine two lines splitting the top into three equal 30° slices.

  4. Fold in the thirds

    Fold the right slice over the middle, then the left slice over the top. Line up the edges carefully.

  5. Trim the top

    Snip straight across the uneven top. This is the Top trim setting in the studio.

  6. Cut & unfold

    Print the cutting template at 100%, trace it onto your wedge, cut every red line, then unfold.

About

A paper snowflake designer that shows the result before you cut

Snowflaker is a free, browser-based tool for designing paper snowflakes. You draw on a picture of the folded paper: a square folded into a 30° wedge with twelve layers. Snowflaker shows the unfolded six-point snowflake instantly, including which pieces fall away. When you like it, print a real-size cutting template or save it as a PDF.

  • Six-fold symmetry. Every cut repeats twelve times, mirrored, just like a real ice crystal.
  • Word snowflakes. Turn a short word or name into cuts that read around all six arms.
  • Plan, then cut. Sketch guides first, trace them into cuts, and import SVG artwork.
  • Private and free. No account and no upload. Designs save automatically in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a six-point paper snowflake?

Fold a square sheet corner to corner, fold the triangle in half, then fold it into thirds so you have a 30° wedge with twelve layers. Trim the top, cut shapes out of the edges and unfold. Real snowflakes have six-fold symmetry, which this fold reproduces. See the folding guide.

Can I preview a paper snowflake before cutting it?

Yes. Draw cuts on the folded wedge and the unfolded snowflake updates instantly. Pieces that would fall away are shown faded, and you can click a piece to keep it.

How do I print a snowflake cutting template?

Choose your paper size and click Print / Save as PDF. Print at 100% (actual size) and check the scale bar. Then trace or tape the template onto your folded wedge and cut along the red lines.

How do I put a word or name in a paper snowflake?

Type up to seven letters into Word cut-out. The fold works as a mirror, so only letters whose top and bottom halves match will work: B, C, D, E, H, I, K, l, O, t and X. The word repeats six times. You can slant the letters, make them straight or curved, and stretch them to fill the arm.

Is Snowflaker free?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser, needs no account, and saves your design automatically on your device. Use Save design to keep a copy as a file.

Can I import my own SVG design?

Yes. Click Import SVG or drop an SVG file onto the folded paper. Its paths become cut lines, or sketch guides if the Sketch layer is active.
